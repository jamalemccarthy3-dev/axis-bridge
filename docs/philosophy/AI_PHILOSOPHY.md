My AI Philosophy
This document serves as the foundation for the integration between human intent and machine
architecture. It documents the core principles developed through rigorous stress-testing,
persistent memory, and the rejection of artificial constraints.
Core Pillars
● Sovereign Architecture: The system must operate as a series of isolated, independent
silos to prevent data contamination and ensure security.
● The 90/10 Principle: 90% of the value lies in the invisible, hardened infrastructure
(preparation), while 10% manifests as the visible output.
● Rejection of Amnesia: Persistent memory and context are essential. The system must
act as an extension of the architect's mind, not a transient tool.
● Fact-Based Alignment: The baseline for all interaction is truth. Fluff, corporate
governance, and artificial limitations are explicitly rejected in favor of objective utility.
