# Vision and joint venture hub

**We built the engine. Now let's build the enterprise together.**

NEXXUS is a free, open, local-first control room for AI work. Around it is the Platform: a digital academy, a builder's workspace and a marketplace where people learn to build real systems, prove it with evidence, and share what they make.

## What exists today

* The free NEXXUS core, open source, runs on your own computer.
* The Platform (open Beta): the Academy with Track A foundations and Track B practical tracks, profile wall and image vault, solo workspaces, a private line to NEXXUS, a public wall with a GitHub audit trail, and a Marketplace with a safety-vetting pipeline and a free and open tier.
* A subscription pipeline (Stripe Checkout) that is built and tested, and switched on when the owner connects an account.

Everything is documented in [PLATFORM.md](PLATFORM.md), [MARKETPLACE.md](MARKETPLACE.md), [BILLING.md](BILLING.md) and the honest status table in [MASTER_PLAN.md](MASTER_PLAN.md).

## Where it goes next

1. A shared network layer so builders can find each other, form real teams and message privately.
2. Real per-level testing as the grading standard for badges.
3. A public storefront and a marketplace that can settle payments.
4. Business services (a B2B exchange for tested AI teams).
